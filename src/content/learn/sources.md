# Sources and limits

## Read and used
1. **RBI, Reserve Bank of India (Bharat Bill Payment System) Directions, 2024** (RBI/DPSS/2023-24/111, 29 February 2024, applicable from 1 April 2024). Full text read at the RBI website, Master Directions page. These Directions supersede the 2014 BBPS circular and later BBPS circulars (para 9). Paragraph numbers in these notes refer to the Annex.
2. **NBBL's website, bharat-connect.com**: home page, Billers page and Operating Units page (including FAQs). Read in November 2026. Figures such as "10+ payment modes" are NBBL's own marketing numbers.

## Could not read
- NPCI's own BBPS pages (npci.org.in) returned no readable content.
- The developer document "TSP Technical Standards v1.0" is public on bharat-connect.com but its text could not be extracted, so API and message names are not covered.
- The RBI circular of 20 September 2019 (turnaround time and compensation) and the RBI online dispute resolution guidelines of 6 August 2020, which the Directions refer to.
- NBBL's operating guidelines and settlement standards. Settlement timelines, settlement file formats and complaint turnaround times therefore appear here as "not found", not as facts.

## Where I mark things as not official
- Anything under "Practitioner notes" or "illustrative" is my own explanation, not a published rule.
- NBBL's pages are not consistent on some points (for example the number of settlement cycles per day) and give no date for the move from "Bharat BillPay" to "Bharat Connect".

## How to correct this repo
If you spot an error, open an issue with a link to the official document. Official documents win over these notes.
